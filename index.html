Jennifer D. Wu

PhD, Political Science

American Politics

Research on race, identity, and political behavior.

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Political Science at Binghamton University.

My research focuses on race, identity, and behavior in American politics. My dissertation project, “The Politics of Asian-ness: Understanding the Importance of National Origin and Group Membership on Political Representation,” examines Asian American political behavior and the conditions under which pan-Asian identity becomes politically unifying despite variation in national-origin backgrounds.

Through studies employing experimental and observational methods, I examine the relative importance of national-origin and pan-ethnic identity, as well as the political contexts in which pan-Asian identity can be activated and made salient.

I received my PhD in Political Science from Yale University in 2023, where I was affiliated with the Institution for Social and Policy Studies and the Center for the Study of American Politics. I received my BA in Economics, Mathematics, and Theatre Arts from the University of Pennsylvania and my MSc in Economics from University College London.
